The house

Fewer homes. Looked after properly.

GreyGrass Stays grew out of living in Goa, not marketing it. We manage a tight collection of houses for owners who want their places kept beautiful, rented with discretion, and handed to guests who will treat them as homes rather than hotel rooms.

The work is unglamorous on purpose: linen, gardeners, well water, laterite dust, guest notes, blocked dates. The result should feel effortless — susegad in the old sense. Quiet. Looked after. Ready when you arrive.

Casa Caisua

The flagship. A century-old laterite house in Anjuna village, restored as a single loft-style suite inside a private orchard. A few minutes’ walk from Vagator beach.

Guest favourite Superhost

Anjuna orchard loft

Guest favourite Superhost

One of the most loved homes on Airbnb, based on ratings, reviews and reliability.

Originally built by architect Raya Shankhwalker’s grandfather and later restored by him, Casa Caisua was featured in Architectural Digest as a study in susegad — unplastered laterite, timber loft, stained cement, and an open-to-sky shower. One bedroom suite, a loft, a verandah of painted columns, and a rear pavilion looking into the trees.

3 guests 1 bedroom + loft 1.5 baths Pet friendly Private kitchen 4.95 · 218 reviews
